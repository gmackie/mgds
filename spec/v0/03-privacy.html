MGDS v0 research preview · normative privacy contract

Private by default, bounded by construction

Local execution is offline and private by default. Collection, persistence, model exposure, evaluation, signing, and publication are separate decisions. A transport or capability MUST NOT bypass the active privacy policy.

Prohibited persisted or published data

ClassRequirement
Credential valuesMUST NOT persist. Use an opaque broker handle and record only capability identity and outcome.
Transcript textMUST NOT enter adapter output, evaluator fixtures, evidence, telemetry, or public results.
Personal identityMUST NOT persist unless the user explicitly selects a documented attribution field for publication.
Raw local pathsMUST NOT cross the project boundary. Replace with project-scoped resource IDs and logical artifact names.
Unrelated sourceMUST NOT be collected. Repository patches and excerpts are restricted to declared task/resource scope.
Unbounded telemetryMUST NOT persist or enter model context. Apply projection, filters, pagination, sampling, aggregation, and byte/item/time limits.

Allowed data classes

  • Public: specification, public task metadata, redistributable golden assets, released schemas and scorecards.
  • Project-scoped: bounded source excerpts, asset/resource identifiers, semantic snapshots, tests, captures, and builds required by a declared task.
  • Evaluator-sealed: hidden assertions, reference outputs, private held-out task variants, and integrity keys.
  • Ephemeral: brokered secret values, raw tool output awaiting redaction, process handles, and temporary paths. Ephemeral data has no persistence permission.

Mandatory processing pipeline

  1. Classify the source and requested fields before collection.
  2. Enforce project/resource scope and observation bounds at the producer.
  3. Normalize identifiers and remove credential, identity, transcript, raw-path, terminal-control, and unrelated-source fields.
  4. Validate the sanitized object against its evidence schema before persistence.
  5. Write atomically to content-addressed storage and record privacy class, producer, transformation, and digest.
  6. Expose only policy-approved projections to the model and evaluator.
  7. Require explicit user selection; the sanitized evidence bundle is previewed before signing or publication.

Redaction MUST occur before persistence. Deleting a leaked value after writing does not satisfy this contract because content-addressed stores, logs, caches, backups, and signatures may retain it.

Retention and deletion

Local unsigned runs default to project-owned retention and MAY be deleted by digest. Published signed bundles are immutable; a withdrawn result publishes a tombstone and reason rather than silently changing history. Ephemeral data is destroyed at job termination or lease expiry. Hidden evaluator material follows the benchmark owner’s separately declared retention policy.

Failure behavior

Unknown privacy class, redaction failure, oversize data, invalid encoding, path escape, or suspected secret produces a terminal privacy error. The system fails closed, preserves only a bounded non-sensitive diagnostic, and does not sign or publish the run.

Conformance evidence

T0 privacy conformance includes synthetic credentials, synthetic local paths, identity fields, transcript-shaped text, terminal escapes, oversized graphs, and unrelated files. Fixtures MUST be demonstrably synthetic and MUST NOT contain developer, customer, host, or internal service details.
