Known limitations

  • The research preview targets Unity 6000.3.9f1 only.
  • Player probing is development-only and is not a production remote administration surface.
  • Accessibility, visual, audio, and multiplayer profiles are research seeds, not comprehensive quality certifications.
  • Human game-feel and creative acceptance remain separate from machine conformance.
  • Cross-platform Unity campaign evidence and independent security review are external release gates.
  • Clean-room P3 implementation proof requires an unaffiliated team.
